01 / SETTLE IN

The notes become
the current.

Each note sends a coloured crest downstream. It widens, leaves a wake and dissolves into the foreground. Play gently, or push the river open.

DAY → DUSK → NIGHT → STORM

Light and insects change together. Daytime cicadas give way to dusk and nighttime crickets. AUTO changes weather every three minutes; GLO page 1 / K3 holds a scene.

Eight fish, one river

Meet the inhabitants

Bluegill and Crappie. Largemouth Bass and Peacock Bass. Brown Trout and Brook Trout. Channel Catfish and Flathead Catfish.

Bait and depth influence the encounter. Each species has its own line drawing and catch records; the musical voice you play does not restrict which fish can visit.

02 / FIND YOUR OWN PACE

Listen. Fish.
Or just play.

RIVER

Leave a line in the water, or lift it and listen. Fish actively or let encounters unfold unattended. Unlock all 27 chromatic keys to take over one musical layer.

FREE PLAY

No automatic composition or fishing. Play the full keyboard. SELECT chooses the FM operator to edit; ALGO chooses the voice. K1–K4 shape ratio, level, feedback and algorithm.

GLO page 4 selects the mode, river key mapping, keyboard voice and octave. SAVE keeps your session.

03 / STEP INTO THE RIVER

Try it here.

The real FM-1 package runs in the existing fm1-emu emulator. Press its Start button, then Space for PLAY. Sound may take a moment to open.

{{PLAYER}}
Computer keyboard & panel controls

Space play/pause · R place/lift/hook · 7 home · 0 fishing/motif

5 FM edit · 3 envelope · 4 LFO · 1 FX · 9 arp · 6 global · 8 save

Left/right arrows: SELECT. Up/down: PRESET. , / .: ALGO. Drag or scroll the panel knobs for K1–K4.

The computer piano keys follow the selected mapping. In FREE, SELECT edits an operator. Use the full emulator for more room. A slow device may stutter at the chip’s 240 MHz.
